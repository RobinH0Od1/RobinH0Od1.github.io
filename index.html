Privacy Policy for ONCE

Effective date: 16 October 2026

ONCE is a minimalist puzzle game by Robin s.p. ("we"), an independent developer in Slovenia. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, and the choices you have. You can play without an account.

On your device

Your level progress, completed daily challenges and settings are stored on your device. They stay there until you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Optional account and cloud save

If you choose to sign in (with email and password, or with Google), we use Firebase Authentication by Google to manage your account. It stores your email address, a user ID and sign-in times. If you sign in with Google, it also stores the name and profile picture Google provides. Your password is handled by Firebase; we never see it.

Your progress is then saved in Cloud Firestore (hosted in the EU) so you can continue on another device: your current level, the dates of daily challenges you completed, whether you have seen the tutorial, and when the save was last updated.

We use this data only to provide the account and cloud save (legal basis: performing our agreement with you). It is kept until you delete your account.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently deletes your account and its cloud save right away. Progress on your device stays.

You can also request deletion without the app.

Usage statistics

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to learn which levels are too hard and where players get stuck. The app records gameplay events (a level started, completed, failed or skipped; tutorial progress; sign-in and sign-up) together with an app instance ID, device model, operating system, app version and approximate country. These events are not linked to your account.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, usage statistics are only collected if you agree in the consent form. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy options. Google keeps this data for a limited period (at most 14 months).

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can fix it: the error details, device model, operating system, app version, a Crashlytics installation ID and the time. Reports are not linked to your account and are kept for 90 days (legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working app).

Advertising

The app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data such as device identifiers (including the Advertising ID), IP address and interaction data to provide personalized or non-personalized ads.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent form asks for your choice before ads are personalized. You can change it at any time under Settings → Privacy options. You can also reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Who receives data

Google provides Firebase and AdMob. Firebase processes data on our behalf; for advertising, Google also acts as an independent controller under Google's Privacy Policy. Some data may be processed outside the EEA; Google protects such transfers with the EU Standard Contractual Clauses. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Your rights

If you are in the EEA or the UK, you have the right to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to withdraw consent at any time. To use these rights, contact us at the address below. You may also complain to a data protection authority; in Slovenia, that is the Information Commissioner (ip-rs.si).

US state privacy rights

If you live in a US state with a consumer privacy law (such as California, Virginia, Colorado or Connecticut), using data for personalized ads may count as "selling" or "sharing" personal information. You can opt out at any time under Settings → Privacy options. You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your data by contacting us at the address below. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Children

This game is not specifically directed to children under 13. Ads are served by Google AdMob in accordance with their policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the effective date above.
