Delete your ONCE account

ONCE is a puzzle game by Robin s.p. Accounts are optional; you only have one if you signed in to save your progress.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ONCE and go to Settings → Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately. If the app asks you to sign in again first, do so and repeat step 2.

Without the app

Email gamesbyrobin@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my ONCE account". We will delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account: email address, user ID, sign-in times, and the name and profile picture from Google if you used Google sign-in.
  • Your cloud save: level progress, completed daily challenges and tutorial status.

What is kept

  • Progress stored on your device stays until you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are not linked to your account. They are deleted automatically: crash reports after 90 days, usage statistics after at most 14 months.
